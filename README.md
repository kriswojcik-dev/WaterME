# Water Me 💧

**A clean water life simulator inspired by charity: water.**

## About the Game

Water Me is an interactive game that demonstrates how access to clean water can impact health, education, and daily life.

Players help Maya manage her hydration, health, energy, and mood while trying to achieve 100% school progress over seven days.

## How to Play

- **Water:** Choose between the river and public pump. Each source requires travel time and has a different risk of sickness.
- **Eat:** Restore health and energy. Limited to three meals per day.
- **School:** Spend eight hours attending school and increase school progress.
- **Rest:** Recover energy and health and begin a new day.
- **Reset Game:** Restart the simulation at any time.

## Features

- Interactive buttons and menus
- Dynamic stat tracking and progress bars
- Game clock and seven-day simulation
- Random sickness events
- School progress penalties
- Results and clean-water comparison
- Reset and replay functionality
- Confetti celebration for reaching 100% school progress
- Responsive layout

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Purpose

This project was inspired by charity: water's mission to provide access to clean and safe water. Through gameplay, players experience how collecting water can take time away from education and how unsafe water can affect daily well-being.

Learn more at [charity: water](https://www.charitywater.org/).

*The game uses fictional probabilities and simplified scenarios for educational purposes.*
